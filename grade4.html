Grade 4 Program

Robotics for Grade 4

An introductory robotics learning experience designed to help young learners explore technology through practical activities and guided projects.

Explore Course Overview expand_more View Grade Projects
Curriculum Foundations

Course Overview

A tailored introduction to technical concepts, encouraging early curiosity and structured thinking in young minds.

school

What Students Learn

Students discover the fundamental building blocks of robotics, including basic electronic components, simple motor operations, mechanical connectivity, and foundational logical flow.

schema

How Course Is Structured

Structured into progressive bite-sized modules starting with basic conceptual assembly and moving gradually into guided team-based activities and basic interactive builds.

construction

Practical Learning Approach

100% hands-on learning model using safe, modular kit components. Students spend over 80% of classroom time physically building, testing, and demonstrating working concepts.

Classroom Engagement

Practical Activities

Guided exercises designed to reinforce core theoretical knowledge through real experimentation.

ACTIVITY 01

Simple Circuit Assembly

Connecting basic LED components, switches, and battery packs to observe open and closed electrical loops.

ACTIVITY 02

Motor & Gear Drive

Assembling simple gear trains and DC motor shafts to understand speed, torque, and directional movement.

ACTIVITY 03

Light & Touch Sensing

Wiring basic touch sensors and photoresistors to trigger sound buzzers or LED indicators upon contact.

ACTIVITY 04

Intro to Visual Logic

Arranging drag-and-drop code blocks to sequence lights, delays, and basic motor commands in order.
