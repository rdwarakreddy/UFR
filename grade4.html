Curriculum Foundations

Course Overview

A comprehensive journey through electronics, mechanical drive systems, and smart sensor integration built to cultivate real-world problem-solving skills.

Electronics & Sensors

Students master essential circuit fundamentals, understanding current, buzzers, DC motors, tilt switches, IR obstacle sensors, soil moisture detectors, and wireless receivers.

Curriculum Roadmap

Course Progression Modules

M1

Basic Electronics & Gear Dynamics

Understanding open/closed circuits, motor rotations, speed/torque trade-offs, and mechanical gears.

M2

Smart Sensor & Alarm Engineering

Building functional alert triggers using distance, water, pressure, weight, LDR light, and soil moisture sensors.

M3

Acoustics & Infrared Telemetry

Mastering sound generation, Morse code keying, and decoding TV remote control IR signals.

M4

Autonomous Mobile Robotics

Assembling self-navigating vehicles including Line Followers, Cliff Avoiders, and IR Remote Control Bots.

Target Competencies

Key Student Outcomes

check_circle

Practical Hardware Integration

Students gain complete independence in connecting motors, buzzers, switches, and multi-pin sensor modules.

check_circle

Logical Thinking & Debugging

Learners discover how to test circuits systematically, identify loose pin joints, and rectify polarities.

check_circle

Real-World STEM Applications

Relating classroom builds directly to modern car sensors, home security systems, and smart irrigation setups.

check_circle

Peer Collaboration & Presenting

Students work in small paired engineering teams, demonstrating their final robotic models to the class.
