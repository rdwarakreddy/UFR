About UniFirst Robotics

Empowering the next generation of engineers, creators, and innovators through practical, hands-on technology education and state-of-the-art robotics infrastructure.

Overview

Who We Are

UniFirst Robotics is a premier educational technology institution dedicated to bridging the gap between standard academic curricula and modern technological requirements.

We specialize in designing and delivering comprehensive STEM programs, robotics lab deployments, microcontroller coding workshops, and specialized technical counseling across schools and higher education institutes.

Background

Our Story

Founded by a team of passionate engineers and educators, UniFirst Robotics started with a simple observation: theoretical learning alone does not prepare students for the rapid advancements in robotics, automation, and artificial intelligence.

To solve this, we developed practical learning kits, industry-aligned curricula, and hands-on lab environments that turn abstract engineering principles into tangible, working technology projects.

visibility
Future Outlook

Our Vision

To become the leading global catalyst for technological literacy, empowering every student with the creative, computational, and practical engineering capabilities required to shape the future of automation.

Our Mission
