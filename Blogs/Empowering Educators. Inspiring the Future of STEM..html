Empowering Educators: UniFirst Robotics Teacher Training Program at Soundarya School

Posted on July 27, 2026 • By UniFirst Robotics • (0) Comments

Empowering Educators. Inspiring the Future of STEM.

At UniFirst Robotics, we believe that building future-ready classrooms begins with empowering the educators who inspire them every day.

As part of our commitment to making STEM education practical, engaging, and accessible, UniFirst Robotics conducted a Teacher Training Program at Soundarya School. The program gave educators an opportunity to strengthen their understanding of robotics and STEM-based learning.

Practical learning for future-ready classrooms

Conceptual understanding, interactive discussions, practical demonstrations, and hands-on exploration formed the core of the program.

From Concepts to Practical Understanding

STEM education becomes more meaningful when concepts move beyond theory and are connected with practical experiences.

Throughout the training, educators were introduced to concepts related to robotics and technology through explanations, demonstrations, and interactive discussions. The sessions encouraged participants to understand not only what a concept is, but also how it works and where it can be applied.

This approach helps bridge the gap between theoretical knowledge and practical understanding, enabling educators to communicate technical concepts to students in a more engaging and relatable way.

Creating an Interactive Learning Environment

One of the key aspects of effective teacher training is creating an environment where learning is a two-way process.

The sessions encouraged educators to actively participate, ask questions, exchange ideas, and discuss different perspectives. Rather than limiting learning to one-way instruction, the program provided opportunities for meaningful interaction between trainers and participants.

Every question can lead to deeper understanding, while every shared perspective can introduce a new way of approaching a concept. The active involvement of educators reflected their enthusiasm to explore new technologies and bring innovative learning experiences into their classrooms.

Moving Towards Future-ready Education

Think, Explore, Experiment

The future of education lies in creating learning experiences that encourage students to think, explore, experiment, and innovate.

Support Continuous Learning

Teacher training programs provide educators with opportunities to continuously learn and confidently embrace evolving technologies.

The Teacher Training Program at Soundarya School was another meaningful step toward strengthening STEM education through collaborative and practical learning. UniFirst Robotics looks forward to continuing its journey alongside educators, supporting innovative teaching practices, and making STEM education engaging, accessible, and impactful for every learner.

“When educators are empowered to explore, experiment, and innovate, they inspire the same spirit of curiosity in every student they teach.”
