Teacher Training & STEM Workshops

Empowering Teachers Through Hands-on STEM Education at Takshasheela School

Posted on July 27, 2026 • By UniFirst Robotics Guest Lecture • (0) Comments

Building Classroom-ready STEM Confidence

The Teacher Training Program was designed to equip educators with the knowledge and practical skills required to confidently deliver Smart Electronics, Robotics, and Coding sessions in the classroom.

Through hands-on learning, real-world applications, and interactive discussions, the program helped teachers explore how STEM education can be integrated into everyday teaching in an engaging and meaningful way.

Program Objectives

Our targeted curriculum ensures teachers gain comprehensive mastery over key STEM components:

inventory_2

Understand the Mini School Kit

Familiarize educators with hardware components and classroom setups.

Explore Smart Electronics

Master fundamentals of circuit building and electronic logic.

sensors

Learn Sensor-Based Applications

Apply real-world sensors to automate everyday interactive tasks.

Learning Beyond the Classroom

One of the most memorable aspects of the program was the learning environment itself. Rather than following a traditional lecture format, every session evolved into an interactive discussion where teachers actively participated, asked insightful questions, shared experiences, and explored new ideas together.

Every project became an opportunity for collaboration, every question opened a meaningful discussion, and every successful circuit brought excitement to the room.

The program reflected a simple principle: the best learning happens when knowledge is shared, not merely delivered.

Hands-on Learning: Bringing STEM to Life

Learning becomes more meaningful when theory is transformed into practice. Throughout the training, educators explored electronics, robotics, sensors, automation, and coding through practical activities.

Building, testing, troubleshooting, and improving projects allowed teachers to experience the same learning process that students will later experience in their classrooms.

Building Confidence Through Collaboration

One of the biggest achievements of the training was the enthusiasm shown by every participant. Teachers collaborated, solved challenges together, exchanged ideas, and supported each other throughout the learning process.

The sessions highlighted that professional development is not only about acquiring new skills. It is also about building a community where educators inspire and learn from one another.

“When teachers continue learning, students continue growing.”

Looking Ahead

Education continues to evolve, and empowering educators is one of the most meaningful ways to prepare students for the future. Programs like these help create classrooms where curiosity, creativity, innovation, and problem-solving become part of everyday learning.

Every discussion, project, and interaction during this training reinforced the value of continuous professional learning.
